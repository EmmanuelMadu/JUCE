Hearth Keys - installing on a Mac
=================================

1. Unzip the download. You get a folder called "Hearth Keys".
2. Open Terminal (Applications > Utilities > Terminal) and type:

       cd ~/Downloads/"Hearth Keys"
       bash install_mac.sh

   (If you unzipped it somewhere else, drag the folder onto the Terminal
   window after typing "cd " instead of typing the path.)

3. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins. Hearth Keys appears as an instrument:
   Audio Unit for Logic Pro and GarageBand, VST3 for Ableton Live, FL Studio,
   Cubase, Reaper, Studio One and others.

The script copies the plug-ins into your own Library folder, removes the
"downloaded from the internet" flag and signs them for this computer. It does
not need an administrator password. The builds are not notarised by Apple,
which is why this step is needed.

"Hearth Keys.app" in the same folder is a standalone version: after running
the script you can open it directly to play without a DAW (choose your MIDI
keyboard under Options > Audio/MIDI Settings).

To remove Hearth Keys, delete:
    ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Hearth Keys.vst3
    ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Hearth Keys.component
