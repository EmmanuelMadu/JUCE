Hearth Keys - grand piano recordings, pack 1
============================================

These recordings are a processed copy of the

    Salamander Grand Piano V3
    by Alexander Holm
    https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3

a Yamaha C5 grand recorded with two AKG C414 microphones, published under
the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported licence (see
LICENSE-CC-BY-3.0.txt in this folder).  The FLAC edition used as the source
is https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano (commit
3382bf9496bba2486f5ab0de55a264d1dfc38404).

Changes made for this pack: the silence before each sound was trimmed, short
fades were added at both ends, every file was scaled to full level (the
instrument restores the original level when it plays), the word length was
reduced from 24 to 16 bits with dither, and the files were renamed.  Nothing
else was done to the sound.  The scripts in tools/grand of the Hearth Keys
repository rebuild this pack exactly from the source.

The pack is passed on under the same licence: you may use, copy and change
it, including commercially, as long as Alexander Holm is credited.
